## Mission Reflection

The Multi-Cloud Explorer mission allowed me to explore and compare three major cloud platforms: AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. The platform that impressed me the most was AWS because it offers many different services and gives organizations several options for building and managing their cloud infrastructure. Through the mission, I learned more about cloud computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and other important services.

One of the main similarities between AWS, Azure, and GCP is that they all provide flexible cloud solutions for businesses. Each platform offers computing resources, storage, databases, networking, identity and access management, and container or Kubernetes services. They also provide scalable resources, which means companies can adjust their cloud environment according to changes in demand.

I realized that choosing a cloud provider depends greatly on the specific needs of a business. A company should consider factors such as cost, performance, security, existing systems, workload requirements, and future expansion. For instance, Azure can be a strong option for businesses already using Microsoft technologies. GCP can be considered by organizations that prioritize AI, machine learning, and data-related workloads. AWS is another strong option for businesses that require a large variety of cloud services.

My experience as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that recommending a cloud platform requires understanding the client first. The best platform is not necessarily the most popular one. A good recommendation should match the client's technical requirements, available budget, current infrastructure, and future objectives.

The mission also made my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio more complete and organized. I added research about cloud platforms, comparisons, decision-making activities, Linux system information, screenshots, and reflections. I also gained more experience with GitHub, Markdown, Linux commands, and technical writing. Overall, this mission helped me develop a stronger understanding of cloud computing and how it can be applied to actual business situations.
